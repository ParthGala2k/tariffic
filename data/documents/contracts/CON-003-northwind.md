# Distribution Agreement

**Between** Brewline Equipment Co. ("Brewline") **and** Northwind Distribution ("Distributor").
**Effective** February 3, 2025.

## 1. Appointment
Brewline appoints Distributor as a non-exclusive distributor of the products in Schedule A in the Mountain West.

## 7. Pricing and Adjustments

**7.1 Prices.** Distributor pays the prices in Schedule A less the distributor discount in Schedule B.

**7.2 Cost Adjustments.** Prices are inclusive of applicable duties, freight and insurance to Distributor's warehouse. Brewline may adjust prices, or add a separately stated charge, to reflect changes in Brewline's landed cost arising from tariffs, duties or similar governmental measures. In the event of any subsequent change to such measures, Brewline will treat Distributor equitably and consistently with its treatment of similarly situated distributors.

**7.3 Notice.** Brewline will give Distributor at least fifteen (15) days' notice of any adjustment under Section 7.2.

## 12. Term
Twenty-four (24) months from the Effective Date.
