# Master Supply Agreement

**Between** Brewline Equipment Co. ("Brewline") **and** Bean Street Coffee Co. ("Customer").
**Effective** January 10, 2025.

## 1. Scope
Brewline sells and Customer buys commercial espresso machines and grinders listed in Schedule A at the prices in Schedule A.

## 7. Pricing and Adjustments

**7.1 Prices.** Prices in Schedule A are fixed for the term, except as provided in this Section 7.

**7.2 Tariff Surcharge.** Where Brewline incurs import duties under any United States tariff measure imposed on or after January 1, 2025 on components of a machine sold to Customer, Brewline may add a separately stated "Tariff Surcharge" line to the invoice for that machine. If Brewline subsequently receives a refund of the duties underlying a Tariff Surcharge, whether by liquidation, protest, court order or otherwise, Brewline shall credit Customer the full Tariff Surcharge billed on the affected machines within thirty (30) days of Brewline's receipt of the refund. Interest received by Brewline on the refund is retained by Brewline.

**7.3 Statement.** Each invoice bearing a Tariff Surcharge shall identify the tariff measure to which it relates.

## 12. Term
Twelve (12) months from the Effective Date, renewing automatically.
