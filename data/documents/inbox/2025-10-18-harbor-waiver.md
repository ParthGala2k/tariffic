# Letter Agreement: Tariff Charges

Harbor Roasters ("Distributor") and Brewline Equipment Co. ("Brewline") agree as follows, supplementing Section 7.2 of the Distribution Agreement dated January 20, 2025:

1. Effective July 1, 2025, Brewline grants Distributor an additional two percent (2%) discount on all Schedule A prices.
2. In consideration of that discount, Distributor waives any claim to a credit, refund or other adjustment of any Tariff Surcharge or tariff-related charge billed by Brewline on or before June 30, 2025, including in the event Brewline later recovers the underlying duties.
3. All other terms of the Distribution Agreement remain in effect.

Signed for Harbor Roasters: M. Okafor, General Manager, June 30, 2025
Signed for Brewline: R. Alvarez, CFO, June 30, 2025
